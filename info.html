What is Saphah Advertising Service?

Saphah Advertising Service provides low-cost advertising placements using standard 125×125 (SQUARE) advertisements.

How it works

Customers provide a link to their 125×125 advertisement image and a link to the page or website they want visitors to reach.

The advertisement is then placed in the Saphah Advertising Service directory once the request and payment have been verified.

Advertising placement

Advertisements remain active until the customer requests cancellation. Cancelled advertisements are removed from the live directory, but their advertising number is never reused.

Important

Saphah Advertising Service provides advertising placement only. No guarantee is made regarding visitors, clicks, traffic, leads, sales, conversions, search rankings, or any other result.

Advertisers are responsible for the content of their advertisements and for the destination pages to which their advertisements link.
